6 Weeks – 12 Months

Infant Care

Our infant program is designed to provide the warmest, most attentive care for your youngest child. We understand that leaving your baby is one of the most significant decisions a family makes — and we take that trust seriously.

Each infant is cared for by a dedicated team member who follows your child's individual schedule for feeding, sleeping, and play. Our nursery environment is calm, clean, and thoughtfully designed to stimulate early sensory development.

Program Highlights

  • Low caregiver-to-infant ratio for personalized attention
  • Individualized daily schedules tailored to each baby
  • Sensory-rich environment to support early brain development
  • Daily communication with families on feeding, naps, and milestones
  • Safe, nurturing nursery designed for infants

Toddler Program

The toddler years are a time of remarkable growth — language explodes, independence emerges, and curiosity becomes insatiable. Our toddler program meets children exactly where they are and gives them the space to explore, discover, and connect.

Through guided sensory play, music, movement, and social interaction, toddlers at South Shore Children's Center build confidence and foundational skills in a warm and structured setting.

4 – 5 Years

Pre-Kindergarten & UPK

South Shore Children's Center is the exclusive UPK provider for the West Islip Union Free School District, selected by the district to host its full-day Universal Pre-Kindergarten program funded by the New York State Education Department.

Our Pre-K program is built around kindergarten readiness — developing the academic skills, independence, and social confidence children need to walk into kindergarten ready to succeed. It is one of the most comprehensive pre-kindergarten programs on Long Island.

UPK — Free Tuition

Qualifying 4-year-olds who are residents of the West Islip school district may be eligible for the UPK program at no tuition cost, funded by the NYS Education Department. Placement is determined by district lottery. Families must register through the West Islip UFSD.

Learn About UPK Enrollment

Curriculum Framework

Our UPK program is grounded in two nationally recognized, evidence-based curriculum frameworks:

  • Heggerty Phonemic Awareness — a structured, sequential program that builds the foundational phonemic awareness skills essential for early reading success
  • Fundations® by Wilson Language Training — a systematic phonics and word study program that develops strong decoding, encoding, and spelling skills from the ground up

Program Highlights

  • Full-day pre-kindergarten curriculum
  • Focus on literacy, numeracy, and critical thinking
  • Social-emotional development and conflict resolution
  • STEM exploration and creative arts
  • Kindergarten transition support for children and families
  • Certified, experienced lead teachers

Kindergarten – 5th Grade

Before & After School Care

Our before and after school program gives working families the peace of mind that their elementary-age children are safe, supervised, and engaged from the moment the school day starts to when parents are ready to pick them up.

We provide a structured environment with homework help, enrichment activities, outdoor play, and healthy snacks — so children arrive home ready to relax, not stressed.

Program Highlights

  • Morning drop-off before school starts
  • Afternoon pickup directly from school
  • Dedicated homework time and academic support
  • Enrichment activities, arts and crafts, and games
  • Outdoor play when weather permits
  • Healthy afternoon snack provided
  • Safe, supervised environment with familiar staff
