Our Story

A Foundation Built on
Trust & Care

South Shore Children's Center of West Islip has been a cornerstone of our community for years — a place where children feel safe, curious, and genuinely celebrated. We believe that early childhood is the most important window in a person's life, and we take that responsibility seriously.

Our dedicated staff creates an environment that blends nurturing warmth with purposeful learning, giving every child the confidence and skills to thrive now and in the years ahead.

Official District Partner

The Exclusive UPK Provider for West Islip Union Free School District

South Shore Children's Center of West Islip is proud to be selected by the West Islip Union Free School District as the sole host site for the district's full-day Universal Pre-Kindergarten program — funded by the New York State Education Department.

UPK provides qualifying 4-year-olds with a free, full-day pre-kindergarten experience in our nurturing, state-of-the-art facility. Placement is determined by district lottery. Contact us or your local elementary school to learn more about eligibility and registration.
