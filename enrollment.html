Our Enrollment Process

We make enrollment as straightforward as possible. Whether you're a returning family or new to South Shore Children's Center, here's how to get started.

01

Schedule a Tour

The best way to get to know us is to visit. Reach out to schedule a tour of our facility and meet our team. We'd love to show you why so many West Islip families have chosen South Shore for over 25 years.

02

Submit an Application

Once you're ready to move forward, we'll guide you through our application. Returning families get priority enrollment each season — see key dates below.

03

Confirm Your Spot

When a spot is confirmed, we'll walk you through all the next steps — paperwork, health requirements, and everything your family needs to feel prepared for day one.

04

Welcome to South Shore

Your child joins a community that will support, encourage, and celebrate them every single day. We can't wait to meet your family.

West Islip UFSD Official Partner

UPK Enrollment

South Shore Children's Center is the exclusive host site for the West Islip Union Free School District's Universal Pre-Kindergarten program. UPK provides qualifying 4-year-olds with free, full-day pre-kindergarten, funded by the New York State Education Department.

Who Qualifies?

Children who turn 4 years old on or before December 1st and reside within the West Islip school district boundaries may be eligible for UPK.

How Does Placement Work?

UPK placement is determined by a lottery administered by the West Islip UFSD, typically held in January for the following school year. Families must register through the district — not directly through our center.

What's the Cost?

UPK is funded entirely by a New York State Education Department grant. There is no tuition cost for families who receive a UPK placement through the district lottery.

To register for UPK, contact the West Islip UFSD Office of Curriculum & Instruction at (631) 930-1559 or visit your local elementary school. For questions about our UPK program specifically, contact us directly.
